# DOCUMENT 09 — RESEARCH SOURCE CATALOG

## 1. Source Discovery Methodology, Epistemic Classification, and Access Provenance

In accordance with Section 3.5 and Section 18 of the Vyom Research Mission, this catalog records only verified primary classical treatises, peer-reviewed scientific and historical studies, and authoritative astronomical and software technical references located and evaluated during research. No citations, URLs, or verse locators have been fabricated. Every entry carries a unique `source_id` referenced by the machine-readable rules in `DOCUMENT_06` and the architectural specifications in `DOCUMENT_01`–`DOCUMENT_08`.

Each source is classified by:
- **Source Category**: `PRIMARY_CLASSICAL_VEDIC`, `PRIMARY_HELLENISTIC_WESTERN_CHINESE`, `TECHNICAL_ASTRONOMY_SOFTWARE`, `SCHOLARLY_HISTORICAL`, or `EMPIRICAL_PSYCHOMETRIC_STUDY`.
- **Access Status**: `FULL_OPEN_TEXT_VERIFIED` (public-domain full text or open-access PDF verified via archive/repository), `METADATA_AND_EXCERPTS_VERIFIED` (bibliographic structure, chapter/verse indices, and excerpts verified), or `LICENSING_SPEC_VERIFIED`.
