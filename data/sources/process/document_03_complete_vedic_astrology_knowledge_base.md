### 1.2 Documented Textual Disagreements on Grahas
1. **Rahu and Ketu Dignities ([BPHS Ch. 3, v. 54](https://archive.org/download/BPHSEnglish/BPHS%20-%201%20RSanthanam.pdf) vs. *Uttara Kalamrita*)**:
   - *School A (Standard Parashari / BPHS Ch. 3, v. 54)*: Exaltation of Rahu is **Vrishabha (Taurus)** and Ketu is **Vrischika (Scorpio)**; Moolatrikona of Rahu is Mithuna (Gemini) and Ketu is Kanya (Virgo); Own signs are Kumbha (Aquarius) for Rahu and Vrischika (Scorpio) for Ketu.
   - *School B (Alternative BPHS variant / South Indian lineages)*: Exaltation of Rahu is **Mithuna (Gemini)** and Ketu is **Dhanu (Sagittarius)**; Own sign of Rahu is Kanya (Virgo) and Ketu is Meena (Pisces).
   - *Vyom Implementation*: Both conventions are stored as explicit configuration flags (`node_dignity_scheme: "taurus_scorpio" | "gemini_sagittarius"`).
2. **Rahu and Ketu Aspects**: Classical early texts (*Brihat Jataka*) do not assign special longitudinal aspects to the shadow planets. Later Parashari commentators assign 5th, 7th, and 9th aspects to Rahu (and sometimes Ketu), while other lineages state Ketu is headless (*Akashiya*) and casts no aspect. Vyom evaluates node aspects only when `enable_node_trinal_aspects: true` is active.

### 1.3 Planetary Relationships: *Naisargika*, *Tatkalika*, and *Panchadha Maitri*
- **Natural Relationships (*Naisargika Maitri*, [BPHS Ch. 3, v. 55](https://archive.org/download/BPHSEnglish/BPHS%20-%201%20RSanthanam.pdf))**: Derived from the sign-rule where lords of the 2nd, 4th, 5th, 8th, 9th, and 12th signs from a planet's Moolatrikona sign (plus its exaltation lord) are natural friends:
  - **Sun**: Friends = Moon, Mars, Jupiter; Neutral = Mercury; Enemies = Venus, Saturn.
  - **Moon**: Friends = Sun, Mercury; Neutral = Mars, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn; Enemies = None.
  - **Mars**: Friends = Sun, Moon, Jupiter; Neutral = Venus, Saturn; Enemies = Mercury.
  - **Mercury**: Friends = Sun, Venus; Neutral = Mars, Jupiter, Saturn; Enemies = Moon.
  - **Jupiter**: Friends = Sun, Moon, Mars; Neutral = Saturn; Enemies = Mercury, Venus.
  - **Venus**: Friends = Mercury, Saturn; Neutral = Mars, Jupiter; Enemies = Sun, Moon.
  - **Saturn**: Friends = Mercury, Venus; Neutral = Jupiter; Enemies = Sun, Moon, Mars.
- **Temporary Relationships (*Tatkalika Maitri*, BPHS Ch. 3, v. 56)**: In a specific chart, any planet occupying the **2nd, 3rd, 4th, 10th, 11th, or 12th sign** counted inclusively from Planet $P$ is a **Temporary Friend (+1)**; any planet in the **1st (conjunct), 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th, or 9th sign** from $P$ is a **Temporary Enemy (-1)**.
- **Five-Fold Compound Relationship (*Panchadha Maitri*, BPHS Ch. 3, v. 57–58)**:
  - Friend ($+1$) + Temporary Friend ($+1$) = **Intimate Friend (*Adhimitra*, $+2$)**.
  - Friend ($+1$) + Temporary Enemy ($-1$) = **Neutral (*Sama*, $0$)**.
  - Neutral ($0$) + Temporary Friend ($+1$) = **Friend (*Mitra*, $+1$)**.
  - Neutral ($0$) + Temporary Enemy ($-1$) = **Enemy (*Shatru*, $-1$)**.
  - Enemy ($-1$) + Temporary Friend ($+1$) = **Neutral (*Sama*, $0$)**.
  - Enemy ($-1$) + Temporary Enemy ($-1$) = **Bitter Enemy (*Adhishatru*, $-2$)**.

## 2. The Twelve Rashis (Signs) and Functional Benefic/Malefic Classification

Each of the 12 sidereal signs (*Rashis*, [BPHS Ch. 4](https://archive.org/download/BPHSEnglish/BPHS%20-%201%20RSanthanam.pdf)) spans $30^\circ$ of sidereal longitude. Moreover, depending on which Rashi rises as the **Lagna (Ascendant)**, each planet acquires a specific **Functional Nature** based on the houses it rules ([*Laghu Parashari* / *Jataka Chandrika*](https://www.astrokarak.com/jyotish-books/book/jataka-parijata)):
- **Trikona Lords (1st, 5th, 9th)**: Always **Functional Benefics (*Shubha*)**.
- **Kendra Lords (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th)**: Natural benefics (Jupiter, Venus, waxing Moon, Mercury) lose their beneficence when ruling Kendras alone (**Kendradhipati Dosha**), whereas natural malefics (Sun, Mars, Saturn) shed their maleficence when ruling Kendras.
- **Yogakaraka**: A single planet that simultaneously rules both a **Kendra (4th, 7th, or 10th)** and a **Trikona (5th or 9th)** becomes the supreme functional benefic for that Lagna.
- **Trishadaya Lords (3rd, 6th, 11th)**: Functional Malefics (*Papa*).
- **Parashraya / Neutral Lords (2nd, 8th, 12th)**: Act according to their other sign lordship and conjunctions; 2nd and 7th lords act as **Marakas** (death/health-afflicting agents in longevity analysis).

## 3. The Twelve Bhavas (Houses) and Reference Points (*Lagnas*)

### 3.1 Bhava Categories and the Three-Pillar Principle (*Bhava, Bhavesha, Karaka*)
Every life domain in Jyotisha is analyzed through three distinct structural components that must never be conflated:
1. **The Bhava (House)**: The spatial container (occupying planets, aspects received, *Bhavamadhya* midpoint strength, *Sarvashtakavarga* bindus).
2. **The Bhavesha (House Lord)**: The ruler of the sign on the Bhava cusp, representing the dynamic agency and trajectory of that house's affairs based on where the lord sits.
3. **The Naisargika Karaka (Natural Significator)**: The universal planetary archetype governing that theme (e.g., Jupiter for children, Venus for marriage, Saturn for longevity). *Rule Exception (*Karako Bhava Nashaya*)*: When a natural benefic Karaka occupies the exact house it signifies alone (e.g., Jupiter in the 5th, Venus in the 7th, Sun in the 9th), classical texts ([*BPHS* Ch. 11–22](https://archive.org/download/BPHSEnglish/BPHS%20-%201%20RSanthanam.pdf); *Phaladeepika*) warn that it can create friction or excess unless supported by the house lord.

### 3.3 Alternative Chart Reference Points (*Sudarshana Chakra* & *Arudha*)
1. **Udaya Lagna (Ascendant)**: Represents the physical body and objective manifestation of events.
2. **Chandra Lagna (Moon as 1st House)**: Represents mental/emotional experience (*Manas*) and social interactions. Particularly vital when birth time is uncertain or when Moon has higher *Shadbala* than Lagna.
3. **Surya Lagna (Sun as 1st House)**: Represents vitality, soul trajectory, and career authority. Combining Lagna, Chandra Lagna, and Surya Lagna forms the **Sudarshana Chakra** ([BPHS Ch. 67](https://archive.org/download/BPHSEnglish/BPHS%20-%201%20RSanthanam.pdf)).
4. **Arudha Lagna ($AL$ / *Pada*, BPHS Ch. 29)**: Represents *Maya*—how the world perceives the native's status.
   - **Exact Algorithm**: Let $H_L \in \{1..12\}$ be the house occupied by the 1st Lord counted from Lagna. Count the same number of signs $(H_L - 1)$ forward from the 1st Lord: $\text{Sign}(AL) = (\text{Sign}(\text{Lagna}) + 2(H_L - 1)) \bmod 12$.
   - **Parashari Exception (*Swasthe Daridra* rule)**: If the resulting $AL$ falls in the **1st house** (when 1st Lord is in 1st or 7th) or the **7th house** (when 1st Lord is in 4th or 10th), shift $AL$ by **10 signs forward** from that preliminary position (so 1st shifts to 10th, and 7th shifts to 4th).

## 4. The 27 Nakshatras (and Abhijit): Mathematical & Symbolic Reference

### 4.1 Mathematical Lookup Algorithm
The sidereal ecliptic $[0^\circ, 360^\circ)$ is partitioned into 27 equal lunar mansions (*Nakshatras*) of $\Delta_{\text{nak}} = \frac{360^\circ}{27} = 13^\circ 20' = 800'$ (arcminutes) each, and $27 \times 4 = 108$ quarters (*Padas*) of $\Delta_{\text{pada}} = \frac{360^\circ}{108} = 3^\circ 20' = 200'$ each:
$$N_{\text{index}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\lambda_{\text{sid}} \times 60}{800} \right\rfloor \in \{0..26\}, \qquad P_{\text{index}} = \left(\left\lfloor \frac{\lambda_{\text{sid}} \times 60}{200} \right\rfloor \bmod 4\right) + 1 \in \{1..4\}$$
Remarkably, the 108 Nakshatra Padas map **1-to-1 continuously** onto the 12 signs of the **Navamsha ($D_9$) chart**:
$$\text{Navamsha Sign Index } (0..11) = \left\lfloor \frac{\lambda_{\text{sid}} \times 60}{200} \right\rfloor \bmod 12$$

## 5. Graha Drishti (Planetary Aspects) and Strength Systems (*Shadbala*)

### 5.1 Sign-Based vs. Degree-Based *Graha Drishti* ([BPHS Ch. 26](https://archive.org/download/BPHSEnglish/BPHS%20-%201%20RSanthanam.pdf))
In Parashari Jyotisha, every planet aspects all houses/signs from its position with fractional strength (*Pada* = quarter of 15 *Virupas*, where 4 Padas = 60 Virupas = 100% full aspect):
- **Standard Planetary Aspect**: All planets cast a $1/4$ (15 Virupas) aspect on the **3rd and 10th** houses, a $1/2$ (30 Virupas) aspect on the **5th and 9th** houses, a $3/4$ (45 Virupas) aspect on the **4th and 8th** houses, and a **full $4/4$ (60 Virupas)** aspect on the **7th** house.
- **Special Full Aspects (*Vishesha Drishti*, BPHS Ch. 26, v. 2–5)**:
  - **Saturn (Shani)** casts a full $4/4$ (60 Virupas) aspect on the **3rd and 10th** houses (in addition to the 7th).
  - **Jupiter (Guru)** casts a full $4/4$ (60 Virupas) aspect on the **5th and 9th** houses (in addition to the 7th).
  - **Mars (Mangala)** casts a full $4/4$ (60 Virupas) aspect on the **4th and 8th** houses (in addition to the 7th).
- **Continuous Degree-Based *Drigbala* (BPHS Ch. 27, piecewise linear equations)**: Let $\Delta\lambda = (\lambda_{\text{aspected}} - \lambda_{\text{aspecting}}) \bmod 360^\circ \in [30^\circ, 300^\circ)$ be the angular separation (*Drishti Kendra*):
  - For $\Delta\lambda \in [30^\circ, 60^\circ)$: $D = (\Delta\lambda - 30^\circ)/2$.
  - For $\Delta\lambda \in [60^\circ, 90^\circ)$: $D = (\Delta\lambda - 60^\circ) + 15$.
  - For $\Delta\lambda \in [90^\circ, 120^\circ)$: $D = 45 - (\Delta\lambda - 90^\circ)/2$.
  - For $\Delta\lambda \in [120^\circ, 150^\circ)$: $D = 30 - (\Delta\lambda - 120^\circ)$.
  - For $\Delta\lambda \in [150^\circ, 180^\circ)$: $D = 2(\Delta\lambda - 150^\circ)$.
  - For $\Delta\lambda \in [180^\circ, 300^\circ)$: $D = (300^\circ - \Delta\lambda)/2$.
  *(Special additions of $+45, +30, +15$ Virupas are added at the exact special aspect zones for Saturn, Jupiter, and Mars).*

### 5.2 Quantitative Six-Fold Planetary Strength (*Shadbala*, BPHS Ch. 27)
Measured in **Virupas** (also called *Shashtiamshas*) and **Rupas** ($1\text{ Rupa} = 60\text{ Virupas}$), *Shadbala* sums six mathematical sub-components for the seven physical planets (Rahu and Ketu are excluded from classical *Shadbala*):
1. **Sthana Bala (Positional Strength)**: Sum of five sub-strengths:
   - *Uchcha Bala* (Exaltation strength): $\frac{|\lambda_{\text{planet}} - \lambda_{\text{debilitation}}|}{3} \in [0, 60]\text{ Virupas}$.
   - *Saptavargaja Bala*: Compound dignity points in $D_1, D_2, D_3, D_7, D_9, D_{12}, D_{30}$ (Moolatrikona = 45, Own = 30, Adhimitra = 22.5, Mitra = 15, Sama = 7.5, Shatru = 3.75, Adhishatru = 1.875 Virupas).
   - *Ojhayugmarashi-amsha Bala*: Moon and Venus gain 15 Virupas each in even Rashi and even Navamsha; Sun, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Saturn gain 15 Virupas each in odd Rashi and odd Navamsha.
   - *Kendradi Bala*: 60 Virupas in Kendra (1, 4, 7, 10), 30 in Panaphara (2, 5, 8, 11), 15 in Apoklima (3, 6, 9, 12).
   - *Drekkana Bala*: Male planets (Sun, Mars, Jup) get 15 Virupas in 1st Drekkana; Neutral (Merc, Sat) in 2nd; Female (Moon, Ven) in 3rd.
2. **Dig Bala (Directional Strength)**: $\frac{|\lambda_{\text{planet}} - \lambda_{\text{powerless\_cusp}}|}{3} \in [0, 60]\text{ Virupas}$, where peak directional points are:
   - **1st House (East / Lagna)**: **Jupiter and Mercury** (powerless in 7th).
   - **10th House (South / MC)**: **Sun and Mars** (powerless in 4th).
   - **7th House (West / Descendant)**: **Saturn** (powerless in 1st).
   - **4th House (North / IC)**: **Moon and Venus** (powerless in 10th).
3. **Kala Bala (Temporal Strength)**: Sum of *Nathonnatha Bala* (diurnal/nocturnal), *Paksha Bala* (lunar phase), *Tribhaga Bala*, *Varsha-Masa-Dina-Hora Bala* (year, month, weekday, and planetary hour lords: 15, 30, 45, 60 Virupas), *Ayana Bala* (declination/tropical), and *Yuddha Bala* (planetary war adjustment).
4. **Cheshta Bala (Motional Strength)**: Derived from heliocentric/geocentric speed and retrogradation (*Vakra* = 60 Virupas, *Anuvakra* = 30, *Vikala* = 15, *Manda* = 15–30, *Sama* = 7.5).
5. **Naisargika Bala (Natural Brightness Strength)**: Fixed constants $\frac{k}{7}\times 60\text{ Virupas}$: Sun $= 60.00$, Moon $= 51.43$, Venus $= 42.86$, Jupiter $= 34.29$, Mercury $= 25.71$, Mars $= 17.14$, Saturn $= 8.57$ Virupas.
6. **Drik Bala (Aspectual Strength)**: $\frac{1}{4}\sum (\text{Benefic Virupas received}) - \frac{1}{4}\sum (\text{Malefic Virupas received})$.
- **Minimum Required Shadbala Thresholds (BPHS Ch. 27, v. 32–33)**:
  - **Sun**: $6.5\text{ Rupas}$ ($390\text{ Virupas}$) | **Moon**: $6.0\text{ Rupas}$ ($360$) | **Mars**: $5.0\text{ Rupas}$ ($300$) | **Mercury**: $7.0\text{ Rupas}$ ($420$) | **Jupiter**: $6.5\text{ Rupas}$ ($390$) | **Venus**: $5.5\text{ Rupas}$ ($330$) | **Saturn**: $5.0\text{ Rupas}$ ($300$).
- **Ishta Phala & Kashta Phala**:
  $$\text{Ishta Phala} = \sqrt{\text{Uchcha Bala} \times \text{Cheshta Bala}} \in [0, 60], \qquad \text{Kashta Phala} = \sqrt{(60 - \text{Uchcha Bala}) \times (60 - \text{Cheshta Bala})} \in [0, 60]$$

## 7. Classical Yogas: Formal Detection, Cancellation, and False Positives

A pervasive flaw in consumer astrology software is firing a celebratory "Rajayoga!" banner whenever two planets share a sign, ignoring degree orbs, combustion, debilitation, or functional maleficence. Detailed machine-readable rules are codified in `DOCUMENT_06`; below is the structural taxonomy of major classical Yogas ([BPHS Ch. 35–41](https://archive.org/download/BPHSEnglish/BPHS%20-%201%20RSanthanam.pdf); [*Phaladeepika* Ch. 6](https://archive.org/details/in.gov.ignca.6945); [B.V. Raman's *Three Hundred Important Combinations*](https://www.scribd.com/document/100615326/Www-bv-Raman-Booklist)):

1. **Pancha Mahapurusha Yogas (*BPHS* Ch. 75, v. 1–2; *Phaladeepika* Ch. 6, v. 1–4)**:
   - **Rule**: Mars (**Ruchaka**), Mercury (**Bhadra**), Jupiter (**Hamsa**), Venus (**Malavya**), or Saturn (**Shasha**) must occupy its **Own Sign (*Swakshetra*), Moolatrikona, or Exaltation (*Uccha*)** AND be placed in a **Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th house) from the Udaya Lagna**.
   - **Disputed Variant**: Some commentators allow Kendra from the Moon (*Chandra Lagna*), but Mantreswara (*Phaladeepika* 6.1) and Parashara assign primary royal status only from Udaya Lagna; Chandra-Kendra placement produces a secondary psychological coloring.
   - **Weakening / Cancellation (*Bhanga*)**: Severe combustion by the Sun (within $5^\circ$), close conjunction with Rahu/Ketu (*Guru-Chandala* cancels *Hamsa*), or debilitation in Navamsha ($D_9$).
2. **Kendra-Trikona Rajayogas (*Laghu Parashari*; *BPHS* Ch. 39)**:
   - **Rule**: Conjunction (*Yuti*), mutual aspect (*Samasaptama Drishti*), or sign exchange (*Parivartana*) between a **Kendra lord ($1, 4, 7, 10$)** and a **Trikona lord ($1, 5, 9$)**—most powerfully the **9th and 10th lords (*Dharma-Karmadhipati Yoga*)**.
   - **False Positive Trap**: If one of the participating planets also rules a *Dusthana* ($6, 8, 12$) or *Badhaka* house without another unblemished connection, the yoga becomes mixed (*Rajayoga Bhanga*).
3. **Dhana Yogas (Wealth Combinations, *BPHS* Ch. 41)**:
   - **Rule**: Mutual relationship (conjunction, aspect, or exchange) among the lords of the **1st, 2nd, 5th, 9th, and 11th houses**.
4. **Gajakesari Yoga (*BPHS* Ch. 36, v. 3–4; *Phaladeepika* Ch. 6, v. 14)**:
   - **Rule**: Jupiter in a Kendra ($1, 4, 7, 10$) from the Moon (or from Lagna in some variants).
   - **False Positive Trap**: Geometrically occurs in $4/12 = 33.3\%$ of all human births! Classical efficacy requires Jupiter not to be debilitated (Capricorn), combust, in an enemy sign, or occupying a Dusthana ($6, 8, 12$) from Lagna, and the Moon to have Paksha Bala.
5. **Kemadruma Yoga and *Kemadruma Bhanga* (*Phaladeepika* Ch. 6, v. 59–61; *Brihat Jataka* Ch. 13, v. 3)**:
   - **Rule**: No physical planet (excluding Sun, Rahu, and Ketu) in the **2nd or 12th sign from the Moon**.
   - **Mandatory Cancellation (*Kemadruma Bhanga*) Checks**: Cancelled if (a) any physical planet occupies a Kendra ($1, 4, 7, 10$) from Lagna, OR (b) any physical planet occupies a Kendra ($1, 4, 7, 10$) from the Moon, OR (c) the Moon is aspected by Jupiter/Venus/Mercury or is full (*Purnima*). Over $85\%$ of apparent Kemadruma formations are cancelled by Kendra planets.
6. **Neechabhanga Rajayoga (Cancellation of Debilitation, *Phaladeepika* Ch. 7, v. 26–30)**:
   - **Rule**: For a debilitated (*Neecha*) planet $P$ in sign $S$:
     1. The dispositor (sign lord) of $S$ is in a Kendra from Lagna or Moon; OR
     2. The planet that gets **exalted** in $S$ is in a Kendra from Lagna or Moon; OR
     3. Planet $P$ is aspected by its dispositor; OR
     4. Planet $P$ is exalted in the **Navamsha ($D_9$)** chart; OR
     5. The dispositor of $S$ and the exaltation lord of $S$ are in mutual Kendras.
7. **Vipareeta Rajayoga (*Phaladeepika* Ch. 6, v. 63–69)**:
   - **Rule**: **Harsha Yoga** (6th lord in 6th, 8th, or 12th), **Sarala Yoga** (8th lord in 6th, 8th, or 12th), and **Vimala Yoga** (12th lord in 6th, 8th, or 12th), provided the Dusthana lord does not simultaneously afflict a benefic Kendra/Trikona lord.

## 8. Vimshottari Dasha and Alternative Dasha Systems ([BPHS Ch. 46](https://archive.org/download/BPHSEnglish/BPHS%20-%201%20RSanthanam.pdf))

### 8.1 The 120-Year *Vimshottari Dasha* Mathematical Specification
Parashara declares *Vimshottari Dasha* (total cycle $120\text{ years}$, equal to the classical *Purna Ayus* human lifespan) as the universal primary Dasha system for *Kali Yuga* ([BPHS Ch. 46, v. 12–16](https://archive.org/download/BPHSEnglish/BPHS%20-%201%20RSanthanam.pdf)).
- **Fixed Planetary Sequence & Period Lengths ($Y_p$)**:
  1. **Ketu**: $7\text{ years}$
  2. **Venus (Shukra)**: $20\text{ years}$
  3. **Sun (Surya)**: $6\text{ years}$
  4. **Moon (Chandra)**: $10\text{ years}$
  5. **Mars (Mangala)**: $7\text{ years}$
  6. **Rahu**: $18\text{ years}$
  7. **Jupiter (Guru)**: $16\text{ years}$
  8. **Saturn (Shani)**: $19\text{ years}$
  9. **Mercury (Budha)**: $17\text{ years}$
  *(Sum: $7 + 20 + 6 + 10 + 7 + 18 + 16 + 19 + 17 = 120\text{ years}$).*
- **Initial Balance of Dasha at Birth (*Bhukta / Bhogya*)**:
  Let $\lambda_{\mathbb{C}, \text{sid}}$ be the Moon's sidereal longitude at birth. Its Nakshatra index is $N = \lfloor \lambda_{\mathbb{C}, \text{sid}} / (40^\circ/3) \rfloor$, which maps to starting lord $p_0 = (N \bmod 9)$ in the sequence above. Let $\Delta\lambda_{\text{rem}} = (N+1)\frac{40^\circ}{3} - \lambda_{\mathbb{C}, \text{sid}}$ be the remaining arc in the Nakshatra (out of $13^\circ 20' = 800'$). The remaining balance of the initial Mahadasha at birth is:
  $$Y_{\text{balance}} = Y_{p_0} \times \frac{\Delta\lambda_{\text{rem}}}{13^\circ 20'} = Y_{p_0} \times \frac{800' - \Delta\lambda_{\text{traversed (in arcmin)}}}{800'}$$
- **Recursive Sub-Period (*Antardasha / Bhukti*, *Pratyantardasha*, *Sookshma*, *Prana*) Formula**:
  At every level $L$, the sub-periods always begin with the **lord of the parent period itself** and cycle through all 9 planets in the standard Vimshottari order:
  $$\text{Duration}(p_1, p_2, \dots, p_k) = \frac{Y_{p_1} \times Y_{p_2} \times \cdots \times Y_{p_k}}{120^{k-1}} \text{ years}$$
- **Year-Length Discrepancy (*Savana* $360\text{d}$ vs. Solar/Gregorian $365.2425\text{d}$)**:
  - **Solar/Gregorian Year ($365.2425$ or $365.25636\text{ days}$)**: Used as the default in almost all modern Vedic software (*Jagannatha Hora*, *Parashara's Light*) so that Dasha transitions align with calendar birthdays.
  - **Savana Year ($360\text{ civil days}$)**: Mentioned in certain classical commentaries; over a 40-year span, a $360$-day year diverges from the $365.2425$-day solar year by **$\sim 210\text{ days}$ ($7\text{ months}$)**. Vyom defaults to `365.2425` days and exposes `360.0` and `365.256363` (sidereal solar year) as explicit user settings.

### 8.2 Conditional Nakshatra & Rashi Dashas ([BPHS Ch. 46–64](https://archive.org/download/BPHSEnglish/BPHS%20-%201%20RSanthanam.pdf))
- **Ashtottari Dasha (108-Year Cycle, 8 Planets excluding Ketu)**: Applicable conditionally ([BPHS Ch. 46, v. 17–21](https://archive.org/download/BPHSEnglish/BPHS%20-%201%20RSanthanam.pdf)) when Rahu is in a Kendra or Trikona from the **Lagna Lord** (without occupying Lagna itself), or for births during daytime in Krishna Paksha / nighttime in Shukla Paksha. Uses the 28-Nakshatra scheme (including Abhijit).
- **Yogini Dasha (36-Year Cycle)**: 8 Yoginis (*Mangala* 1y, *Pingala* 2y, *Dhanya* 3y, *Bhramari* 4y, *Bhadrika* 5y, *Ulka* 6y, *Siddha* 7y, *Sankata* 8y). Starting Yogini index $= (\text{Nakshatra\_1based} + 3) \bmod 8$.
- **Jaimini Chara Dasha (Sign-Based)**: Operates on the 12 Rashis rather than Nakshatra lords; period length of each Rashi equals the count of signs from the Rashi to its lord (counted zodiacally for *Vishama Pada* signs $1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 9$ and anti-zodiacally for *Sama Pada* signs $4, 5, 6, 10, 11, 12$).

## 9. Transits (*Gochara*), *Ashtakavarga*, and *Panchanga*

### 9.1 Classical Transit Houses from the Natal Moon (*Phaladeepika* Ch. 26)
In traditional *Gochara*, transits are evaluated primarily from the **Natal Moon Sign (*Janma Rashi*)** (and secondarily from Udaya Lagna), modified by **Vedha (Obstruction)** points:
- **Sun** is favorable in houses **3, 6, 10, 11** from Natal Moon (Vedha points: 9, 12, 4, 5 respectively; note Sun and Saturn do not cause Vedha to each other).
- **Moon** is favorable in **1, 3, 6, 7, 10, 11** (Vedha: 5, 9, 12, 2, 4, 8).
- **Mars** is favorable in **3, 6, 11** (Vedha: 12, 9, 5).
- **Mercury** is favorable in **2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11** (Vedha: 5, 3, 9, 1, 8, 12).
- **Jupiter** is favorable in **2, 5, 7, 9, 11** (Vedha: 12, 4, 3, 10, 8).
- **Venus** is favorable in **1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 11, 12** (Vedha: 8, 7, 1, 10, 9, 5, 11, 6, 3).
- **Saturn** is favorable in **3, 6, 11** (Vedha: 12, 9, 5).
  - **Sade Sati ($7.5$-Year Saturn Transit)**: When transiting Saturn traverses the **12th, 1st (*Janma*), and 2nd signs** from the Natal Moon (~$2.5\text{ years}$ per sign).
  - **Dhaiya / Ashtama & Kantaka Shani ($2.5$-Year Transit)**: Saturn transiting the **4th (*Ardhashtama*) or 8th (*Ashtama Shani*)** sign from the Natal Moon.

### 9.2 Ashtakavarga (*Bhinna* & *Sarvashtakavarga*, BPHS Ch. 66)
- Each of the 7 physical planets ($P \in \{\text{Sun}..\text{Saturn}\}$) has a **Bhinna Ashtakavarga (BAV)** table scoring $0$ to $8$ benefic points (*Bindus* / *Rekhas*) in each of the 12 signs based on placements relative to the 7 planets plus Lagna:
  - Total BAV Bindus: Sun $= 48$, Moon $= 49$, Mars $= 39$, Mercury $= 54$, Jupiter $= 56$, Venus $= 52$, Saturn $= 39$.
- **Sarvashtakavarga (SAV)**: The sum of the 7 BAV tables across all 12 signs has a constant grand total of **$337\text{ Bindus}$** (average $28.08\text{ Bindus/sign}$). Signs with $\ge 30\text{ SAV Bindus}$ yield strong resilience and material results during transits; signs with $< 25\text{ Bindus}$ experience strain.
- **Kakshya (Sub-Zone) Transit Timing**: Each $30^\circ$ sign is divided into 8 *Kakshyas* of $3^\circ 45'$ each, ruled in decreasing orbital order by **Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon, and Lagna**. When a transiting planet traverses a Kakshya whose lord contributed a *Bindu* (1) in that planet's BAV, the transit yields auspicious results during that $\sim 3^\circ 45'$ window.

### 9.3 Panchanga and Electional (*Muhurta*) Formulas ([*Muhurta Chintamani*](https://archive.org/details/muhurt-chintamani-of-shri-ram-daivajna-with-pt.-kapilessvar-shastri-185-haridas-sanskrit-series))
Given apparent sidereal longitudes of the Sun ($\lambda_{\odot}$) and Moon ($\lambda_{\mathbb{C}}$):
1. **Tithi (Lunar Day, 1..30)**: Each Tithi spans $12^\circ$ of synodic elongation:
   $$T_{\text{index}} = \left\lfloor \frac{(\lambda_{\mathbb{C}} - \lambda_{\odot}) \bmod 360^\circ}{12^\circ} \right\rfloor + 1 \in \{1..30\}$$
   ($1..15 = \text{Shukla Paksha}$ culminating in *Purnima* [15]; $16..30 = \text{Krishna Paksha}$ culminating in *Amavasya* [30]).
2. **Vara (Solar Weekday)**: Begins at **local sunrise** (not civil 00:00 midnight!), governed by Sun (*Ravivara*), Moon (*Somavara*), Mars (*Mangalavara*), Mercury (*Budhavara*), Jupiter (*Guruvara*), Venus (*Shukravara*), Saturn (*Shanivara*).
3. **Nakshatra (1..27)**: $\lfloor \lambda_{\mathbb{C}, \text{sid}} / 13^\circ 20' \rfloor + 1$.
4. **Yoga (Luni-Solar Sum, 1..27)**: *Vishkambha* to *Vaidhriti*:
   $$Y_{\text{index}} = \left\lfloor \frac{(\lambda_{\mathbb{C}, \text{sid}} + \lambda_{\odot, \text{sid}}) \bmod 360^\circ}{13^\circ 20'} \right\rfloor + 1 \in \{1..27\}$$
5. **Karana (Half-Tithi, 1..60)**: Each $6^\circ$ of $(\lambda_{\mathbb{C}} - \lambda_{\odot}) \bmod 360^\circ$:
   $$K_{\text{index}} = \left\lfloor \frac{(\lambda_{\mathbb{C}} - \lambda_{\odot}) \bmod 360^\circ}{6^\circ} \right\rfloor + 1 \in \{1..60\}$$
   (4 Fixed Karanas: *Shakuni* [58], *Chatushpada* [59], *Naga* [60], *Kimstughna* [1]; 7 Movable Karanas cycling 8 times across indices $2..57$: *Bava, Balava, Kaulava, Taitila, Gara, Vanija, Vishti/Bhadra*).
6. **Rahu Kalam, Yamaganda, Gulika Kalam, and Abhijit Muhurta**:
   Let local daytime duration be $D_{\text{day}} = t_{\text{sunset}} - t_{\text{sunrise}}$, divided into 8 equal segments of $\Delta_8 = D_{\text{day}} / 8$:
   - **Rahu Kalam Segment Index (1..8)** for Sun..Sat: **[8, 2, 7, 5, 6, 4, 3]** (Mnemonic: *"Mother Saw Father Wearing The Very Hat"*).
   - **Yamaganda Segment Index (1..8)** for Sun..Sat: **[5, 4, 3, 2, 1, 7, 6]**.
   - **Gulika Kalam Segment Index (1..8)** for Sun..Sat: **[7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1]**.
   - **Abhijit Muhurta**: The 8th of 15 daytime *Muhurtas* ($\Delta_{15} = D_{\text{day}} / 15$), centered on local apparent solar noon: $[t_{\text{noon}} - \frac{D_{\text{day}}}{30},\; t_{\text{noon}} + \frac{D_{\text{day}}}{30}]$.
