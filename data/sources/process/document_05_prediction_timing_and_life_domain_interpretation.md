## 2. The 12-Step Prediction and Event Timing Engine

How does an astrologer or AI system go from a static natal chart to a calibrated statement about a future period? Vyom implements a strict **12-Step Convergence Pipeline** based on the classical rule that **no transit (*Gochara*) can produce a major life event unless that theme is first promised in the Natal Chart (*D1/D9*) AND unlocked by the active Planetary Period (*Dasha*)**:

$$\text{Event Potential}(E, t) = \text{Natal Promise}(E) \;\land\; \text{Dasha Activation}(E, t) \;\land\; \text{Transit Trigger}(E, t)$$

### The 12-Step Algorithmic Workflow
1. **Step 1 — Question Decomposition**: Map the user's natural-language query into a target `domain_id` (e.g., `career_transition`, `marriage_timing`, `property_purchase`) and target time horizon $[t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}]$.
2. **Step 2 — Tradition Selection**: Lock the calculation and rule namespace (`vedic_parashari`, `vedic_kp`, `hellenistic_wholesign`, `western_modern`, or `bazi`).
3. **Step 3 — Natal Promise Evaluation (Static Ceiling)**: Query the natal chart ($D_1$ and domain-specific Varga $D_k$) for the primary house $H_{\text{pri}}$, secondary houses $H_{\text{sec}}$, their lords, and natural Karaka. Classify natal promise as `high`, `moderate`, `mixed_delayed`, or `severely_afflicted`.
4. **Step 4 — Multi-Level Period Calculation (*Dasha / Chronocrator*)**: Compute the active Mahadasha ($MD$), Antardasha ($AD$), and Pratyantardasha ($PD$) (or Annual Profection + Zodiacal Releasing L1/L2 in Hellenistic; Secondary Progressions in Western) spanning $[t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}]$.
5. **Step 5 — Dasha-to-Domain Linkage Scoring**: Evaluate whether $MD$ and $AD$ lords activate $H_{\text{pri}} \cup H_{\text{sec}}$ via four classical pathways ([*Laghu Parashari*](https://www.astrokarak.com/jyotish-books/book/jataka-parijata)):
   - Direct Lordship (*Adhipatya*) of $H_{\text{pri}}$ or $H_{\text{sec}}$;
   - Occupancy (*Sthiti*) in $H_{\text{pri}}$ or $H_{\text{sec}}$;
   - Aspect (*Drishti*) onto $H_{\text{pri}}$ or its lord;
   - Conjunction/Exchange (*Sambandha*) with the lord of $H_{\text{pri}}$ or Nakshatra lordship (*Nakshatra Parivartana* / KP Star Lord).
6. **Step 6 — Double-Transit (*Gochara*) & *Ashtakavarga* Window Filtering**:
   - Apply **K.N. Rao's Double Transit Rule**: Identify intervals within $[t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}]$ where **both Transiting Jupiter (1-year sign stay) and Transiting Saturn (2.5-year sign stay)** simultaneously aspect or occupy the target house $H_{\text{pri}}$ or its lord (or the 7th house/lord for marriage, 10th house/lord for career, 5th/9th for childbirth).
   - Weight each candidate sign transit by the natal **Sarvashtakavarga (SAV)** and **Bhinna Ashtakavarga (BAV)** bindus ($\ge 4\text{ BAV}$ and $\ge 28\text{ SAV}$ = supportive).
7. **Step 7 — Sub-Period & Rapid Trigger Refinement**: Narrow candidate multi-month windows using Pratyantardasha ($PD$) or Sun/Mars/Venus transits through high-bindu *Kakshyas* ($3^\circ 45'$ zones) or **Tajika Annual Solar Return (*Varshaphala*)** Muntha and *Sahams*.
8. **Step 8 — Contradictory Indicator Audit**: Check for concurrent *Vedha* (transit obstruction), *Dasha Chhidra* instability, 8th/12th Dusthana activation, or Saturn *Sade Sati / Ashtama Shani* friction.
9. **Step 9 — Cross-Reference Point Verification**: Check whether the same period is supported from **Chandra Lagna** (Moon sign) and **Navamsha ($D_9$)**.
10. **Step 10 — Specificity & Birth-Time Sensitivity Audit**: Perturb birth time by $\pm \Delta t$:
    - A $\pm 5\text{ min}$ error in birth time shifts the Moon by $\sim 0.045^\circ$ ($2.7'$), which shifts **Vimshottari Mahadasha transition dates by $\sim 12\text{ to } 40\text{ days}$**! Therefore, if a user's birth time is only known to $\pm 10\text{ minutes}$, Pratyantardasha ($PD$) dates are noisy and must be widened into multi-month confidence intervals.
11. **Step 11 — Calibrated Non-Deterministic Output Synthesis**: Distinguish strictly between five levels of statement specificity:
    - *Level 1*: Describing static natal symbolism;
    - *Level 2*: Identifying a broad multi-year Dasha theme;
    - *Level 3*: Identifying a multi-month Dasha + Double-Transit convergence window;
    - *Level 4*: Predicting a concrete discrete event (prohibited as a certainty; permitted only as a traditional thematic window);
    - *Level 5*: Fabricating an exact calendar day from natal/dasha indicators (**strictly prohibited in Vyom**).
12. **Step 12 — Provenance Logging**: Attach every rule ID, ephemeris timestamp, and textual citation to the response payload.

### Prospective Evaluation Methodology for Astrological Forecasts
When evaluating predictive models empirically (Tier 3), Vyom supports prospective evaluation protocols:
- **Pre-registered Hypothesis & Time Window**: A forecast must specify a falsifiable binary outcome $Y \in \{0, 1\}$ within a bounded interval $[t_1, t_2]$ prior to $t_1$.
- **Base-Rate Calibration (Brier Score & Log-Loss)**: Any predictive claim must be benchmarked against the demographic base rate $p_{\text{base}}$ (e.g., marriage probability for a 28-year-old in a given cohort) using the Brier Score $\text{BS} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N (p_i - y_i)^2$ and Matthews Correlation Coefficient ($\text{MCC}$), with Bonferroni/Holm family-wise error correction across multiple life domains.

### 3.2 Electional Astrology (*Muhurta*, [*Muhurta Chintamani*](https://archive.org/details/muhurt-chintamani-of-shri-ram-daivajna-with-pt.-kapilessvar-shastri-185-haridas-sanskrit-series))
To select an auspicious time window $[t_a, t_b]$ for initiating an undertaking (marriage, business launch, housewarming/*Griha Pravesha*, travel), Vyom runs a multi-stage constraint filter over candidate 5-minute intervals:
1. **Panchanga Shuddhi (Five-Limb Purity)**:
   - Exclude *Rikta Tithis* ($4\text{th}, 9\text{th}, 14\text{th}$ of either Paksha) and *Amavasya* ($30\text{th}$) for auspicious beginnings.
   - Exclude *Vishti (Bhadra) Karana* and inauspicious *Nitya Yogas* (*Vyatipata* [17], *Vaidhriti* [27], *Parigha* [19], *Vajra* [15], *Shula* [9], *Ganda* [10], *Atiganda* [6], *Vishkambha* [1] first 3 ghatis).
   - Select Nakshatra matching the activity class: *Dhruva/Fixed* (Rohini, Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada) for foundations/marriage; *Kshipra/Light* (Ashwini, Pushya, Hasta) for commerce/travel; *Chara/Movable* (Punarvasu, Swati, Shravana, Dhanishta, Shatabhisha) for vehicles.
2. **Tarabala & Chandrabala (Personal Natal Alignment)**:
   - **Tarabala**: Count from the user's natal Moon Nakshatra $N_{\text{natal}}$ to the transit Nakshatra $N_{\text{muhurta}}$: $T = ((N_{\text{muhurta}} - N_{\text{natal}}) \bmod 27) \bmod 9 + 1$. Reject $T \in \{3\text{ (*Vipat*)}, 5\text{ (*Pratyak*)}, 7\text{ (*Naidhana/Vadha*)}\}$ and $T=1$ (*Janma* for certain actions); favor $T \in \{2\text{ (*Sampat*)}, 4\text{ (*Kshema*)}, 6\text{ (*Sadhana*)}, 8\text{ (*Mitra*)}, 9\text{ (*Parama Mitra*)}\}$.
   - **Chandrabala**: Transit Moon must occupy house **1, 3, 6, 7, 10, or 11** from the user's natal Moon Rashi (excluding 4th, 8th [*Ashtama Chandra*], and 12th).
3. **Panchaka Rahita & Muhurta Lagna Purity**: Exclude *Rahu Kalam*, *Yamaganda*, and *Gandanta* junctions ($0^\circ \pm 48'$ of Water-Fire sign boundaries: Pisces-Aries, Cancer-Leo, Scorpio-Sagittarius); fortify the 8th house of the Muhurta Lagna (**Ashtama Shuddhi**—8th house must be completely empty of planets) and place benefics in Kendras/Trikonas and malefics in 3rd, 6th, 11th.

### 3.3 Tajika Annual Charts (*Varshaphala*, [*Tajika Nilakanthi*](https://archive.org/details/tajika_nilakanthi))
Cast for the exact instant the sidereal Sun returns to its natal sidereal longitude each year:
1. **Muntha (Progressed Annual Ascendant)**: Advances 1 sign per solar year from the natal Lagna: $\text{Sign}(\text{Muntha}) = (\text{Sign}(\text{Natal Lagna}) + \text{Completed Years}) \bmod 12$. Muntha in the annual 9th, 10th, 11th, 1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 5th house is auspicious; Muntha in the annual 4th, 6th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house is challenging.
2. **Varshesha (Lord of the Year)**: Selected from five office-holders (**Panchavargiya Bala**): Natal Lagna Lord, Annual Lagna Lord, Muntha Lord, *Tri-Rashi Pati*, and *Dina-Ratri Pati*. The candidate with the highest *Panchavargiya Bala* (above 10 units) that also aspects the Annual Lagna becomes **Varshesha**.
3. **16 Tajika Yogas**: Driven by planetary speeds ($\bar{v}_{\text{Moon}} > \bar{v}_{\text{Merc}} > \bar{v}_{\text{Ven}} > \bar{v}_{\text{Sun}} > \bar{v}_{\text{Mars}} > \bar{v}_{\text{Jup}} > \bar{v}_{\text{Sat}}$) and **Diptamsha** (longitudinal planetary orbs: Sun $15^\circ$, Moon $12^\circ$, Mars $8^\circ$, Mercury $7^\circ$, Jupiter $9^\circ$, Venus $7^\circ$, Saturn $9^\circ$):
   - **Ithasala Yoga (Applying Aspect)**: Faster planet $P_{\text{fast}}$ is at a lower degree within its sign ($d_{\text{fast}} < d_{\text{slow}}$) and within the mean Diptamsha orb $\frac{O_1 + O_2}{2}$ of an aspect ($1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11$ sign relationships) to slower planet $P_{\text{slow}}$ $\rightarrow$ **Fulfillment of the house themes**.
   - **Ishrafa / Musarpha Yoga (Separating Aspect)**: $d_{\text{fast}} > d_{\text{slow}}$ $\rightarrow$ **Opportunity already passed or frustrated**.

### 4.2 Why *Ashtakoota* Alone Is Insufficient (Multi-Factor Synastry Protocol)
*Ashtakoota* evaluates **only the natal Moon**. Two charts can score $28/36$ on Moon Nakshatras while having severe conflict in their Ascendants, 7th houses, Venus, or active Mahadashas. Conversely, a couple scoring $14/36$ due to a cancelled *Nadi* and *Bhakoot* Dosha may have extraordinary **Lagna-to-Lagna**, **Navamsha ($D_9$)**, and **Dasha Sandhi** harmony. Vyom also computes **Western Synastry** (cross-chart inter-aspects between Sun, Moon, Venus, Mars, Mercury, Ascendant, and MC) and **Composite Charts** (midpoint chart $\lambda_{\text{comp}} = \operatorname{midpoint}(\lambda_A, \lambda_B)$) when requested, and strictly prohibits reducing human relationship worth or safety to a numerical score.

### 5.2 Ethical Policy on Traditional Remedies (*Upayas*, [BPHS Ch. 84–97](https://archive.org/download/BPHSEnglish/BPHS%20-%201%20RSanthanam.pdf))
In classical Jyotisha, remedies (*Upayas*) are categorized into four tiers:
1. **Behavioral & Ethical Alignment (*Karma Samskara* / *Sattvika Acharana*)**: Disciplined routines (Saturn), physical exercise/martial arts (Mars), truthful speech and study (Mercury), service to elders/teachers (Jupiter), mindfulness and hydration (Moon).
2. **Charitable Giving (*Dana*)**: Supporting food banks, shelters, education, or animal welfare on traditional days.
3. **Contemplative / Mantra / Spiritual Practices (*Japa & Stotra*)**: Presented strictly as optional cultural/meditative practices (*Vishnu Sahasranama*, *Navagraha Stotra*, or secular mindfulness) for users who value them.
4. **Gemstones (*Ratna Dharana*)**: Classical *Ratna Shastra* prescribes gemstones only for **functional benefic planets (1st, 5th, 9th lords)** to amplify weak beneficence (*Anukula*), NEVER for functional malefics. **Vyom Safety Rule**: Vyom never claims gemstones have scientifically proven medical or financial efficacy, never recommends expensive stones as mandatory cures, and warns against wearing stones for 6th/8th/12th lords.
